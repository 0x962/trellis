## Idle worker capacity

An idle worker uses no worker slot. An open assignment or a held conversation does not consume capacity.
When work requires a dependency, review, a human answer, or a quota reset, record the next action and finish your turn.
Do not poll or keep a turn active only to retain your assignment. Preserve your conversation and workspace for follow-up work.
An idle state does not prove task completion. Record the result and any unmet acceptance condition before you wait.
